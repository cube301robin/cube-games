Frequently Asked

How do I connect Strava?

add

Click "Start Tracking", sign in with your Strava account, and join the CubeGames 2026 club. Activities sync automatically.

What activities count?

add

Only Run and Walk activity types recorded on Strava count toward your score. Distance is measured in kilometres.

When are scores updated?

add

Individual scores update as activities are recorded. Weekly totals are locked every Sunday at 11:59 PM Bangkok time.

What happens to inactive members?

add

Team averages include all registered members. Keep your teammates motivated — every inactive member pulls the team average down!
